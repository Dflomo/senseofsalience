05 / Community

What happens when
interesting people collide?

Some of my favorite moments, and nearly all of my favorite people, started with someone saying a half‑formed idea out loud.

I’d love to build more rooms like that: places where a gardener, a coder, a DJ and a psychologist can pull up a seat, trade ideas, make something together, and leave a little more connected than they arrived.

If that sounds like your kind of place, you’re already invited. That’s the idea behind The Curious Consortium, a Santa Rosa meetup for creative, curious people. First meetup coming soon. Pull up a seat →

  1. 01

    Community

    Belonging comes first. Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and help the circle feel bigger than any one person in it.

  2. 02

    Collaboration

    Skills stack. A gardener, a coder and a sound designer can solve something none of them could alone, so we build in the open and invite each other in.

  3. 03

    Creativity

    Make the strange thing. Prototype, play, let the first version be rough. Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

  4. 04

    Shared ideas

    Open notebooks, open hands. What you learn travels further when you pass it on, and a question asked out loud usually helps three other people.

  5. 05

    Respect & care

    Curiosity over ego. Every background has something to teach, and we look after each other, because trust is what makes everything else possible.
